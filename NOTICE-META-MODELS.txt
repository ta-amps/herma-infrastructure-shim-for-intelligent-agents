WHICH MODELS, AND WHERE

Through the Meta Model API
- Muse Spark 1.3 and Muse Wonder 1.0: research, building and review by our live workers.
- Muse Spark 1.3 Contributor: benchmarks and test work on our public queue. Meta may use what is sent to this tier to train its models. Live Eranos requests use the standard tier.
- Muse Code: one recorded build test, October 31, 2026. Live workers do not use it.

Hosted by Baseten
- Muse Spark Open: helps our always-on agent choose a model for real Tasks. It was also the teacher in one private training run on November 2, 2026. The small model from that run is private and is not served.

Open weights we run ourselves
- Muse Glimmer 30B: we fine-tuned it with small adapters. One adapter is in small live use, in batches, on a private GPU.
- Llama 3.2 1B Instruct and Llama 3.2 3B Instruct: private fine-tuning tests. They are not in any live service.
- SAM (facebook/sam-vit-base) and wav2vec 2.0 (facebook/wav2vec2-base-960h): local quality checks on an earlier film, now retired.
- MusicGen (facebook/musicgen-small): music for that retired film, and music comparisons. No film on our sites uses it.
- Contriever (facebook/contriever): one private test, November 2, 2026. It is not in any live service.

Named but not run by us
- Some examples, recipes and research notes in our repositories name other Meta open models, such as Llama 3.1 8B, Llama 3.3 70B, Llama 4 Maverick and Llama Prompt Guard. We do not run them.

What we distribute
- We do not distribute Meta's model weights or weights made from them. Our public repositories hold settings files and test results only. If that changes, the license copies those licenses ask for will ship with the weights.
